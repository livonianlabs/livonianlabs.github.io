FlipSky privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

FlipSky is a flip clock and weather app with home screen widgets, made by Livonian Labs (Riga, Latvia).

Contact: livonianlabs@gmail.com

In short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your location is used only to get the weather for where you are. It is rounded to about 1 km before it leaves your phone, and we never receive or store it.
  • Your settings stay on your phone.

What the app uses and why

Location. If you allow it, FlipSky reads your phone's approximate location (it never asks for precise location) while the app is open, to show the weather where you are. You can instead pick a city by hand, and the app then never reads your location. The coordinates are rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) and sent to the weather services below. FlipSky does not read your location in the background.

City search and place names. Typing a city name, or turning coordinates into a place name, uses Android's built-in geocoder. On most phones it is provided by Google Play services.

Weather data. To fetch weather, the app sends the rounded coordinates to:

  • MET Norway (api.met.no), for the forecast. MET Norway terms
  • Livonian Labs' air quality files on GitHub Pages (livonianlabs.github.io/flipsky-air), for air quality in Europe. The app downloads the file for its 1° square (about 100 km), so only that rough area is revealed. GitHub privacy statement
  • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (services.swpc.noaa.gov), for space weather. No location is sent there.

Like any web service, these services see your IP address when the app connects. FlipSky sends no other personal data. The requests name the app and our contact address, as MET Norway requires.

Stored on your phone. Your settings and the last downloaded forecast are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Purchases. FlipSky Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We get only a confirmation that the purchase exists, never your payment details.

What we don't do

We don't collect, sell or share personal data. There is no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no account.

Children

FlipSky is a general audience app. It is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
