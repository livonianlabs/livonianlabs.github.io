Quiet Walls privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Quiet Walls is a live wallpaper app made by Livonian Labs (Riga, Latvia).

The app collects no personal data and sends nothing from your device. It asks for no permissions and has no ads, analytics or accounts.

Your wallpaper settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Android's own device backup may include them if you have backup turned on.
