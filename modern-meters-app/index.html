The gadgets

Five gadgets, everything at a glance

CPU

Total load and its graph, clock, uptime, memory, every core (P- and E-cores apart) or every thread, temperatures (Core Temp fetched in two clicks), the five busiest programs a click away.

GPU

Load and temperature rings, video memory, memory controller, power, fan, clocks, temperature, encoder and decoder, PCIe link; NVIDIA read directly, others through DirectX.

Network

Download and upload with a graph, Wi-Fi and signal, addresses (hidden until clicked), firewall, link, ping, traffic totals.

Drives

Every disk with its partitions, health, SSD life left, temperature, read and write speed.

Weather

Now, the next hours as a chart, rain in the next two hours, a rain map for the next 12 hours, three days, sunrise and sunset, UV and air quality (MET Norway; Copernicus over Europe).

Good to know

Install, updates, privacy

Install

  1. Download ModernMeters-…-win-x64.zip from the latest release.
  2. Unpack it into a folder of your choice and run ModernMeters.exe. The first start lines the gadgets up and asks whether to start with Windows.
  3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway, as for any new program that isn't signed yet.

Everything is in the menu of the tray icon by the clock: gadgets, line them up, style, accent color, glass transparency, language, always on top, animations, start with Windows. Drag a gadget by its title; ⚙ and ✕ appear under the mouse.

New versions

Once a day the app looks for a new version. It shows as a small ↑ on the gadgets' titles. In the copy from the Microsoft Store a click installs it, and the app starts again by itself; in the copy from here a click opens its page to download it.

Privacy

No tracking. The app connects to MET Norway for the weather, livonianlabs.github.io for air quality, OpenStreetMap for place names, api.ipify.org for your external IP, 1.1.1.1 for the ping, alcpu.com for Core Temp when you ask for it, and GitHub (the Store copy: the Microsoft Store) for the check for a new version. The glass styles read your wallpaper to draw it; it stays on your computer, as do the settings.
