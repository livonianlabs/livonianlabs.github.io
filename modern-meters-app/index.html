Modern Meters

Beta 0.9

CPU, GPU, Network, Drives and Weather gadgets for Windows 10 and 11, each in its own small window on the desktop. No 8GadgetPack needed.

Download the latest version

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Unpack the zip anywhere and run ModernMeters.exe.

The gadgets

  • CPU — total load and its graph, clock, uptime, memory, every core (P- and E-cores apart) or every thread, temperatures (Core Temp fetched in two clicks), the five busiest programs a click away
  • GPU — load and temperature rings, video memory, memory controller, power, fan, clocks, temperature, encoder and decoder, PCIe link; NVIDIA read directly, others through DirectX
  • Network — download and upload with a graph, Wi-Fi and signal, addresses (hidden until clicked), firewall, link, ping, traffic totals
  • Drives — every disk with its partitions, health, SSD life left, temperature, read and write speed
  • Weather — now, the next hours as a chart, a rain radar map, rain in the next two hours, three days, sunrise and sunset, UV and air quality

A live zone at the right of every graph shows the last seconds five times a second, so short spikes show at once. Alarms sound when the processor or graphics card overheats, video memory is nearly full or the connection drops. The gadgets line up along the right edge by themselves and stay on the desktop when you press Show desktop.

Install

  1. Download ModernMeters-…-win-x64.zip from the latest release.
  2. Unpack it into a folder of your choice and run ModernMeters.exe. The first start lines the gadgets up and asks whether to start with Windows.
  3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway, as for any new program that isn't signed yet.

Everything is in the menu of the tray icon by the clock: gadgets, line them up, style, accent color, glass transparency, language, always on top, animations, start with Windows. Drag a gadget by its title; ⚙ and ✕ appear under the mouse.

New versions

Once a day the app looks for a new version. It shows as a small ↑ on the gadgets' titles; a click opens its page to download it.

Privacy

No tracking. The app connects to Open-Meteo for the weather, RainViewer and Esri for the radar map, api.ipify.org for your external IP, 1.1.1.1 for the ping, alcpu.com for Core Temp when you ask for it, and GitHub for the check for a new version. The glass styles read your wallpaper to draw it; it stays on your computer, as do the settings.
